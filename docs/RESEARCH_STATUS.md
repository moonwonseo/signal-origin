# Research status

Latest completed historical experiment: V4, 3 November 2026. Publication preparation: 12 November 2026. This work packages existing methods and creates a synthetic public demonstration; it does not introduce V5 or a new market finding.

| Stage | Completed work | Interpretation |
| --- | --- | --- |
| V1 | Chronological B0/B1/A1 comparison on a +10% endpoint event at 63 sessions | The initial weather representation did not improve the price baseline. |
| V2 | Changed only the target to any daily close within 63 sessions | Temporary crossings matter, but overlapping origins are not distinct shocks or measured purchasing losses. |
| V3 | Annual expanding refits and a dry-spell representation | No stable weather advantage was established. |
| V4 | Added freight changes; tested 63/21 sessions and two availability assumptions | Combined inputs helped the full longer-horizon average, with unfavorable recent-period, shorter-horizon and timing checks. |

Compare models within an unchanged target, sample and fitting policy. Do not interpret score changes across these stages as a single continuous improvement measure. The recent subset is contained in the full sample, not an independent replication.

The original inputs represented vendor Coffee C closes, one NASA POWER pilot point near Varginha/Sul de Minas, and a broad US freight-cost proxy via FRED/BLS. They did not establish coffee-specific vessel traffic, port delays, crop damage, physical invoice prices or procurement savings. No additional shipping source was acquired in publication preparation.

Seasonality-only baselines, separately validated calibration, coffee-specific stock/export/logistics inputs, crop-stage interactions and historical freight-vintage reconstruction remain proposed. They must not be described as completed tests or demonstrated fixes.

The public demo demonstrates model fitting and review mechanics with artificial inputs. Its rankings and numbers are not evidence for the historical conclusions above. Original numerical tables and historical viewers remain local pending source-use review.
