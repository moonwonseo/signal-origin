03 / The research question

Can weather and freight context improve price-risk estimates?

The original exploratory V4 study found mixed evidence: combined inputs helped the full longer-horizon replay, but did not establish a stable advantage across recent dates, shorter horizons and timing checks. This public demo does not reproduce those numerical results.

The repository preserves the modeling methods. Independent confirmation, calibration changes, coffee-specific logistics inputs and verified historical vintages remain future work.

04 / Keep the limitations visible

Artificial inputs, real software mechanics.

Prices: a seeded mathematical path, with no vendor observations or exchange-calendar records.

Weather: generated daily history; preserved prior-year anomaly and dry-spell calculations, aligned at t−4 calendar days.

Freight: a generated monthly index with assumed +180-day or +45-day availability.

Evaluation: annual expanding fits, training-only scaling, complete matured labels and matched samples. No market-performance, causal or buyer-savings claim.
