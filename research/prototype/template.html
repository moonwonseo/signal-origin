How far to trust the number

The weather panel is context, not a forecast input. We tested whether six-region weather, crop-stage timing or fund positioning sharpen this number. None did, in direction or in turbulence. Forecasts may help a buyer see risk sooner, but there are too few frost events to prove it.

The gauge measures turbulence, not direction, and it overstates risk in calm years.
